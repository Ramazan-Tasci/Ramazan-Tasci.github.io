About Me

My interest in software development began around the age of 13–14, before I started high school. Inspired by the games I played, I became curious about whether I could create my own games. I started researching the subject, reading articles, watching tutorials, and experimenting with game development on my own. Although my young age and limited English made the learning process difficult at the time, those experiments became my first introduction to software development.

After taking a few years away from programming, I returned to it and started learning C#. I built small, text-based applications and game-like projects in the terminal to test my knowledge and experiment with different concepts. During this period, I realized that I genuinely enjoyed writing code and creating functional things that were completely my own. I later returned to game development with Unity and worked on several small projects, including a 2D platformer.

Today, I focus on web development and freelance work. I use HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap to build modern, responsive websites while developing my freelance career. I built this portfolio as part of that journey, and I plan to keep improving it as I gain more experience. My next goal is to learn JavaScript, add more functionality to the websites I build, and eventually expand my skills into backend development.
